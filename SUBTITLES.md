# Subtitles: what is said, and what is on screen

Every subtitle of the seven clips: its time in the clip, and the words on screen. For the French clips, the French
sentence said is next to its English translation. Use it to check while watching.

- **English clips** show what is said, word for word. Left out: fillers (“uh”, “um”) and repeated words. Kept as
  said: small slips (“From my interviews”, “We submit”, “advices”, “requirement process”). Written as the intended
  word where the accent makes the sound ambiguous (“falling behind schedule”, “years of experience”).
- **French clips** show a faithful English translation of each sentence.
- **Times** come from aligning the exact words on the sound of each clip (forced alignment), checked against where
  the voice starts and stops. No word is said without its subtitle on screen.
- **Not shown**: the name of the school in scene 3 (“final-year student at …”), which could not be made out.
